Camera and mic

You opened PageCue straight from its folder, and browsers often block or keep re-asking for the camera there. For recording, close this tab and double-click Start-PageCue.bat (Windows) or Start-PageCue.command (Mac) in the PageCue folder. It opens PageCue at http://localhost:8765, where the camera works and your browser remembers your choice. Your saved story doesn't carry over by itself: use Save project file here, then Open project file there.

Your browser will ask first. Nothing is uploaded.

Retake key

Stumbled? Press R. PageCue goes back to the start of that line, counts down while it keeps recording, and cuts the slip from the video. Press it again to retry again. Many presentation clickers send B or a period from their "blank screen" button.
